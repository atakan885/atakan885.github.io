IMPLEMENTED WORK

What I’ve built

ALICE — an interactive HoloLens AR application

I developed an AR application that lets learners explore a complex detector, select individual subsystems and connect its digital structure to a physical LEGO model. I turned a technical system into an interactive spatial learning experience.

Published work & evidence

Çoban, A., Girdhar, A., Warkentin, M., Hoyer, C., Weller, J., Kuhn, J., Koenigstorfer, S., Fabbietti, L., & Mehlhase, S. (2024). Bringing CERN to classrooms: Learning about ALICE with AR and LEGO. The Physics Teacher, 62(4), 310–311.

DOI: 10.1119/5.0203650

INDUSTRY APPLICATIONS I CAN DEVELOP

What I can build for your organisation

PROPOSED WORKPLACE LEARNING EXPERIENCE

01

Equipment exploration

Learners inspect an interactive equipment model, select components and explore their functions.

02

Guided inspection

Learners follow a defined inspection task with explanations and prompts linked to the relevant parts.

03

Repeatable task practice

Learners repeat the task in a simulated workplace. Task checks show where they need further practice.

Interactive 3D & AR modules

Let learners explore equipment, select components and see how parts contribute to a process. Add explanations and task prompts to make the model a learning activity.

Immersive VR scenarios

Create virtual workplaces, guided walkthroughs and task-based simulations for onboarding or technical familiarisation, adapted to the target headset and interaction requirements.

Reusable learning activities

Build structured scenes with clear objectives, interactions and checks for understanding. Working with subject-matter experts, develop a series of modules for different products, tasks or learner groups.

Example application

A new technician explores a machine in AR, selects a component to understand its function, then follows a guided inspection task. A VR extension can place the same task in a simulated workplace for repeatable practice.

Potential deliverables: Interactive 3D scenes · AR applications · VR learning scenarios · Guided activities · Learner instructions · Task-level evaluation

How I would evaluate value

Evaluate independent task completion, critical-step errors and support needs. Relevant KPIs include time to proficiency, error rate and rework rate.
