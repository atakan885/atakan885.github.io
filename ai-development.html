AI-Assisted App & Learning Content Development

From a learning idea to an interactive experience: websites, learning apps and practice activities developed with AI assistance.

IMPLEMENTED WORK

About the project

AI-assisted websites, applications & interactive designs

I use AI tools such as OpenAI and Claude to turn ideas into working websites, applications and interactive designs. I have developed a range of these experiences, including this portfolio. I define the requirements, guide development through clear prompts, review the output and refine the content, interface and interactions.

Potential applications

I can apply this AI-assisted development approach to learning experience design: interactive web modules, decision scenarios, simulations and practice apps. I translate learning objectives into meaningful tasks, choices and feedback, then use AI tools to prototype and refine the experience. My learning-science background helps me evaluate both the generated content and the quality of the learning design.
