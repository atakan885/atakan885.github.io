Problem-Based Learning Design

My work

Designed quantum physics cases that ask learners to compare explanations and justify conclusions.

Workplace use

Run case workshops where teams use evidence to make and explain a decision.

How I designed the cases

For my master’s research, I developed quantum physics cases that begin with a problem learners cannot solve immediately. They identify what they need to know, investigate possible explanations and defend a conclusion using evidence.

Workplace applications

A team could investigate recurring customer complaints using case notes, workflow data and sample messages. Participants decide what information is missing, compare possible causes and propose a response they can explain to colleagues.

How to evaluate it

Use a rubric to review the evidence selected, the alternatives considered and the justification for the proposed action. A new case can show whether learners apply the same reasoning independently.
