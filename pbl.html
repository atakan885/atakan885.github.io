IMPLEMENTED WORK

What I developed

Problem-Based Quantum Physics Learning

I designed the learning materials, problem situations and teaching activities for my master’s research. The learning units addressed topics where learners struggled to connect explanations with meaningful situations.

?Start with a problem.

The learning experience

The problem gives learners a reason to investigate. They identify what they need to know, discuss explanations and use what they have learned to propose a solution.

Evidence & evaluation

My master’s research evaluated conceptual understanding and critical-thinking attitudes. The thesis abstract reports no significant change in critical-thinking attitudes. The portfolio therefore presents the design experience without claiming an improvement in that measure.

How I can apply this experience

I can design learner-centred workshops and case-based learning around workplace problems. Each case can require evidence, a decision and a clear explanation of the proposed action.

PROPOSED WORKPLACE APPLICATION

PROPOSED WORKPLACE LEARNING EXPERIENCE

01

Investigate a case

Participants examine a workplace case, identify what is known and decide what information they need to investigate the problem.

02

Compare explanations

Participants compare possible explanations, discuss the evidence and consider the consequences of different responses.

03

Justify an action

Participants propose an action and explain their reasoning. A facilitator reviews the decision against defined criteria and gives feedback.

Example scenario

A team receives a case about a recurring customer complaint. Participants identify missing information, compare possible explanations and justify an action plan. A facilitator uses defined criteria to review the reasoning.

Potential deliverables: Problem scenarios · Case materials · Facilitator guides · Discussion prompts · Assessment criteria

Potential KPIs

Quality of decisions · Use of evidence · Quality of proposed actions
